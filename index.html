Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have clear answers to help you start with complete confidence.

How do online 1-on-1 Quran classes work?
Classes are conducted live over Zoom or Google Meet with interactive screen-sharing of Noorani Qaida and Quran pages. The student and dedicated teacher interact in real-time, allowing immediate phonetic corrections and encouragement.
Can I request a female teacher for my daughter or myself?
Yes, absolutely. We have dedicated, certified female Quran teachers (Qarias) exclusively for sisters, teenage girls, and young children. Male teachers are assigned to brothers and boys.
What happens during the Free Trial Class?
The free introductory session allows the teacher to assess the student's current recitation level, introduce the curriculum, and let you evaluate the audio/video quality and teaching methodology with zero payment obligations.
What if we need to reschedule a class due to illness or travel?
We offer flexible make-up classes! Simply inform your teacher or the WhatsApp academic coordinator at least 4 to 6 hours before class, and we will schedule a convenient make-up session.
What devices and equipment do I need?
You only need a stable internet connection and any laptop, desktop, iPad, tablet, or smartphone equipped with a microphone and webcam.
What payment methods do you accept for monthly fees?
We accept safe international payments via Credit/Debit Cards (Visa/Mastercard), PayPal, Stripe, Wise, Bank Wire, and local Pakistani banking/JazzCash/EasyPaisa.
